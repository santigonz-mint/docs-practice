title: "Favorite Soccer teams"
---

These favorite soccer teams are grouped three ways: by the country they play in, by the league they compete in, and by the kind of fan base that follows them. Use the sections below to browse whichever way you prefer.

## By Country

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="England">
    - **Manchester United**: Record-setting English champions based at Old Trafford.
    - **Liverpool**: Anfield club known for its European Cup history.
    - **Arsenal**: North London club famous for the 2003-04 "Invincibles" season.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Spain">
    - **Real Madrid**: The most decorated club in European Cup history.
    - **FC Barcelona**: Catalan club known for its possession-based style and La Masia academy.
    - **Atlético Madrid**: Madrid's other giant, known for its intensity and defensive grit.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Italy">
    - **Juventus**: Turin club with the most Serie A titles.
    - **AC Milan**: Seven-time European champions sharing the San Siro.
    - **Inter Milan**: Milan's other powerhouse and the 2010 treble winners.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Germany">
    - **Bayern Munich**: Germany's dominant club and multiple-time European champions.
    - **Borussia Dortmund**: Known for its famous "Yellow Wall" stand.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="France">
    - **Paris Saint-Germain**: The capital's club and France's most successful modern side.
    - **Olympique de Marseille**: The only French club to win the Champions League.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="South America">
    - **Boca Juniors** (Argentina): Plays at the iconic La Bombonera.
    - **River Plate** (Argentina): Boca's fierce rival in the Superclásico.
    - **Flamengo** (Brazil): Rio de Janeiro club with a massive following.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
